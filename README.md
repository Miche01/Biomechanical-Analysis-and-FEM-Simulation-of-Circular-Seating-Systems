# Biomechanical Analysis and FEM Simulation of Circular Seating Systems
Research scholarship | Design and Validation |   
Project OverviewThis project is part of the Circular Sofa Platform, developed within the MICS (Made in Italy Circolare e Sostenibile) program. The goal is to design a sustainable, modular alternative to traditional polyurethane (PU) foam using circular elastomeric thermoplastic polymers.The system utilizes modular cells designed for manual assembly and easy disassembly to support a circular economy model.My Role: FEM Analyst & Biomechanical ResearcherI managed the technical-operational workflow focused on the digital validation of these innovative padding prototypes using Abaqus CAE 2022.1. FEM Simulation & Experimental ValidationI conducted extensive static load analyses to compare numerical results with physical prototype tests.Setup: Replicated physical test conditions (400x400x150 mm modules) using a rigid base support.Calibration: Defined material properties for TPU (Shore 90A/D41), treating it as a linear elastic material with an elastic modulus of 90 MPa and Poisson's ratio of 0.48.Optimization: Iterated through different assembly constraints—including Tie (bonding) and General Contact (explicit/standard)—to identify the most realistic mechanical behavior.Accuracy: The final simulation achieved a vertical displacement of 39 mm, within a 10% error margin of experimental results (35 mm).2. Anatomical Modeling & User InteractionTo simulate realistic sitting conditions, I integrated advanced virtual anatomical models into the FEM environment.Model Selection: Utilized the THUMS (Total Human Model for Safety) AM50 (50th percentile male) model developed by Toyota.Computational Optimization: Simplified the 2.1-million-element model by sectioning it at the navel to focus on the gluteal region, reducing calculation time and memory usage.Material Assignment: Assigned location-specific biomechanical properties to tissues:Bones: 17.3 GPa (Isotropic).Soft Tissue (Flesh): 1 GPa (Isotropic).Skin: 1 GPa (Orthotropic).Load Mapping: Applied pressures proportional to Body Weight (BW) at key anatomical landmarks: Ischial Tuberosities (18%), Thighs (21%), and Sacrum (5%).Key ResultsSuccessfully validated the use of Explicit Dynamic solvers to manage complex buckling deformations in the padding structure.Generated pressure distribution maps showing peak stresses at the ischial tuberosities (approx. 5 kPa on skin), providing a foundation for predictive comfort modeling.
